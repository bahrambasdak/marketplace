# Feature Gate

## Preconditions
Approved spec, risk level, focused design, and known dependencies.

## Checklist
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are observable.
- [ ] Scope and out-of-scope behavior are clear.
- [ ] Required data/API/UI behavior is implemented.
- [ ] Success, loading, empty, error, and authorization states are handled.
- [ ] Focused tests and diagnostics pass.
- [ ] Relevant docs and knowledge are updated.

## Evidence
Spec checklist, design notes, test commands/results, review findings, and changed-file scope.

## Failure Conditions
Unresolved acceptance criteria, untested important behavior, hidden errors, scope drift, or stale documentation.

## Reviewer
Product Mentor plus the responsible implementation role; Quality/Security for medium and above.

## Exit Criteria
All applicable checklist items pass and residual risk is recorded.
